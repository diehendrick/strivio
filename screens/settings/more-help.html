Help & Support

Getting Started

How does plan generation work?
Strivio generates a personalized plan based on your onboarding answers, including goals, preferences, and fitness level. The plan includes workout schedules and nutrition targets tailored to you.
How do I log food?
Go to the Logbook tab and tap the + button. You can search for foods, scan barcodes (Premium), or manually enter nutrition data.
What is the workout schedule?
Your workout schedule is generated based on your preferred training days and frequency. You can view it in the Workouts tab on the Home screen.

Premium

What features are Premium?
Premium features include barcode scanning, AI photo estimation, custom meals, data export, and advanced analytics.
How do I upgrade or cancel?
Tap your membership status in More to manage your subscription. You can upgrade anytime or cancel before the next billing cycle.

Troubleshooting

Notifications not working?
Check that notification permissions are enabled in your device settings. Go to Settings > Strivio > Notifications and ensure they are allowed.
Health sync issues?
Ensure Apple Health or Google Fit has granted Strivio permission to read your data. Try a manual sync from More > Sync Data.
